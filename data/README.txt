CERBERUS-X: Synthetic Employee Verification Dataset
100 synthetic cases, combining PDF profiles and offer letters, scanned employee ID images, payslip photos, and CSV attendance/payroll records.
Distribution: 15 consistent; 20 single conflict; 30 multiple conflicts; 15 missing evidence; 10 unreadable evidence; 10 unsupported employment claims.
Each case folder includes annotation.json with expected findings. Global annotations are in annotations/master_annotations.csv.
All names, companies, salaries, employee IDs and records are fictional. Use for prototype/testing only. This dataset is not suitable for real hiring, employment, or identity decisions. A consistent case does not prove a person is genuine; request independent verification where needed.
